# EcoNeTool 1.7.7 - Epifaunal Bivalves

Released 2026-10-20.

### Fixed — bivalve environmental position (EP)

- **Zebra mussels and other epifaunal bivalves are epibenthic (EP3).** A bivalve with no habitat text fell to the
  all-bivalves rule and was made endobenthic (EP4). The common mussels, oysters and scallops were rescued by BIOTIC's
  "attached" / "free_living" text, but species missing from BIOTIC were not, most importantly the zebra mussel
  (*Dreissena polymorpha*) and Conrad's false mussel (*Mytilopsis leucophaeata*). Epifaunal families now get EP3:
  Mytilidae, Dreissenidae, Ostreidae, Gryphaeidae, Pectinidae, Anomiidae, Limidae, Arcidae, Pinnidae, Spondylidae
  and Chamidae.
- Rock-boring date mussels (*Lithophaga*, *Leiosolenus*, *Botula*) stay EP4, and habitat text still decides first.
- The rules sit behind the existing "infaunal bivalves" switch in the harmonization settings, whose label now says so.

### Upgrade notes

- Cached trait lookups are refreshed automatically (the rules are part of the trait vocabulary hash).
- No configuration or data changes. The offline trait DB does not use these rules and needs no rebuild.
