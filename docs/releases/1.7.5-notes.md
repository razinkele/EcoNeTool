# EcoNeTool 1.7.5 - Zooplankton Orders

Released 2026-10-20.

### Fixed — trait lookup

- **Water fleas, mysids and krill now query MAREDAT.** WoRMS ranks these as orders (not classes) inside
  Branchiopoda and Malacostraca, so the class-based router never sent them to the zooplankton database, although
  it holds 16 cladocerans, 7 mysids and 15 krill. The router now also looks at the WoRMS order. WoRMS has no
  "Cladocera" rank, so water fleas are matched by their four orders (Anomopoda, Ctenopoda, Onychopoda, Haplopoda);
  clam shrimps, which share their superorder, are not routed. The invertebrate sources stay on, and amphipods,
  crabs and isopods are unchanged.

### Upgrade notes

- No configuration, data or database changes. No offline trait DB rebuild needed.
