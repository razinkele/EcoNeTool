# EcoNeTool 1.7.10 - Survey Completeness

Released 2026-10-21.

### Fixed — DATRAS survey data says when it is incomplete

- **A DATRAS outage is no longer shown as "species absent".** When every request timed out or failed, the lookup
  returned "no DATRAS rows", which the Trait Research tab presents as genuine absence. It now reports
  "DATRAS unavailable: N request(s) failed or timed out". A web-service outage reported by icesDatras also counts.
- **Partial results are no longer cached.** A series with some years timed out was kept for the life of the server
  process and reused for every later user. Only complete results are cached now, so a later request retries.
- **Survey-trend validation leaves out incomplete series.** A demersal group whose BITS lookup was incomplete is
  excluded with the reason "incomplete survey data (timeouts)", instead of fitting a trend on the remaining years.
- **The Trait Research tab flags partial data** with a "DATRAS: partial data (N request(s) failed or timed out)"
  warning, and the status line notes it.
- **ICES SAG timeouts are named as such:** "SAG timed out ..." instead of "no SAG assessment for ...".

### Changed

- `lookup_datras_indices()` results carry two new fields: `complete` (TRUE when no request failed) and `failures`
  (one entry per failed request, e.g. "BITS 2022 Q1: timeout").

### Upgrade notes

- No configuration, data or database changes.
